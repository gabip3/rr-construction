One team.
Every part of your renovation.

Most renovations stall because too many people are involved and nobody owns the schedule. We handle demolition, framing, trade coordination, tile, cabinetry and finish work with one crew and one point of contact, so you always know who to call.

Kitchen Remodeling

Cabinet layouts built around how you actually cook, countertops templated to the millimeter, and tile that lines up where it should. We handle the plumbing and electrical rough-in so the room comes back together on schedule.

  • Cabinetry and countertops
  • Islands and layout changes
  • Backsplash and tile work
  • Lighting and appliance rough-in

Custom Carpentry & Trim

Built-ins, panelled walls, stair details and open shelving cut and fitted on site. This staircase wall started as plain drywall and carpeted treads. Every miter is cut to the wall it lands on, because no room in an older Atlanta house is truly square.

  • Built-ins and shelving
  • Panelled and shiplap walls
  • Stairs, rails and balusters
  • Crown, casing and baseboard

Built around the way you actually live.

R&R Construction works with Atlanta homeowners who like where they live and want the house to work better. Most of our projects are additions to daily life rather than replacements for it. A kitchen that fits a family of five. A basement that becomes an office. A bathroom that finally drains the way it should.

We work in occupied homes most of the time, so we plan around school runs, work calls and the parts of the house you still need to use. You get the same crew from demolition through final walkthrough, and one person who answers the phone.
